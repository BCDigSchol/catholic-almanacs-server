The works. Every record whose type is a school, academy, college, asylum, home, hospital or infirmary, once an institution a year; parishes and religious houses alone are left out.

Paying and free. Paying: academies, colleges and boarding schools by their type, and any work whose note speaks of select or pay schools, boarders, board and tuition, or terms by the year, session or quarter. Free: free schools ("free school", "gratuitous", "without charge"). Some kept both ("select and free schools"). Most parish schools say neither; many charged small fees and excused the poor, which the almanacs don't record.

Charity. Asylums, homes and refuges, by whom their name or note says they took in: orphans, boys sent to reform or industrial schools, women in Good Shepherd and Magdalen refuges, the aged, infants and foundlings, servants and working girls, the poor. Hospitals are counted apart: they took paying and charity patients alike.

Churches. What a church's note says it was built of (stone, brick, frame or wood, log). Few notes say, so this follows the parishes the almanac chose to describe, not every parish.

What this cannot show. There is no census here: these are the institutions' own descriptions, not the incomes of the people in them. Fees and costs are printed for a handful of institutions only and are shown as examples. A fee by the month is counted as ten months, by the session as two, by the quarter as four. These markers are read from every record each time the data is loaded, so records added later are read the same way; notes in wording the patterns don't know are listed on the Data Issues page.
