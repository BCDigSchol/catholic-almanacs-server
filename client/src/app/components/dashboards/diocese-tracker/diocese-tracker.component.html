Each point is a place the almanac recorded that year, snapped to a ~0.1° grid and sized by how many institutions it had. Dioceses are matched by their canonical id (the prefix of each institution's id), so a see recorded under different names over the years stays one diocese, shown under its latest name.

Colours are assigned largest diocese first, so the biggest sees get the most distinct colours; with more dioceses than colours, some smaller sees share one. Counts in the key are for the selected year; dimmed dioceses have nothing recorded that year.

The dashed lines are the dioceses' territories as they stood on 1 January of the year shown, drawn at Boston College from the almanacs and other sources; where a diocese is picked, its territory is shaded. They are a work in progress: fuller in the east, drawn for some years and carried forward until a diocese's next change, so a few lines may lag a change or be missing in the west.

"Outside their diocese’s territory" places every record with a location in the territory drawn for its year and compares it with the diocese the almanac listed it under. A disagreement can be a misplaced record, a parish attended across a diocesan line, or a territory still to be corrected; the CSV lists them for checking.

Years with no almanac data (the 1862–63 Civil War gap, and years not yet transcribed) are skipped by the player and drawn as a break on the time axis.
