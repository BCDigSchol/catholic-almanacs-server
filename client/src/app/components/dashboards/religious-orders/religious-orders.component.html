The almanacs seldom tie an order to an institution, but they print a member's order after the name: S.J. for the Jesuits, C.SS.R. for the Redemptorists, O.S.B., O.S.F., C.M., O.P. and so on. A person counts as a member of the order any of their listings marks, so a Jesuit printed once without "S.J." still counts, and an order's footprint is every place its members were listed that year. Titles that are not orders (D.D., V.G.) are not counted.

Women's congregations are seldom marked this way in the almanacs, so they are mostly missing here; the orders named on institutions are too few to chart.
