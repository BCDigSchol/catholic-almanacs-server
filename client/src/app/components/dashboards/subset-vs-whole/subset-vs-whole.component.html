Both lines count the same kind of institution: the whole is every one recorded in the almanac that year; the part is those in the chosen state, diocese or religious order.

Indexed growth rebases each line to 100 in the base year, so a part and a much larger whole can be compared on one scale; 150 means half again as many as in the base year. The default base is the start of the latest unbroken run of years, so growth isn’t distorted by the long gap in coverage.
