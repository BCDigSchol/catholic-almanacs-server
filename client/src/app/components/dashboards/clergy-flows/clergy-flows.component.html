A move is read from the almanacs year by year: between two years a priest (or brother or sister) is listed, they left one resident post and took up another. Keeping a post while adding one (a pastor who also became a college's president), or leaving without a new post, is not a move. A move counts in the diocese each post was listed under that year, so a parish that later passed to a new see (Rochester, from Buffalo, in 1868) counts in the old one until it changed, and in the year it arrived.

The almanacs skip 1839–1863. A priest listed in 1838 at one post and in 1864 at another moved somewhere in between: those {{ data?.totals?.gapMoves ?? 0 }} moves are left out unless you include them. Visits to stations are not moves (see Mission Circuits).
