Focus on any person or institution (search above, or Focus here in a node's details) to see its neighbourhood from the whole network, not just the most central nodes: one step out, two, or its whole connected group. The network falls apart into separate groups; the list under the graph opens the largest of them.

Hexagons are institutions and dots are people. A line joins a person to each institution where they are listed within the selected years; its number and thickness count the years of that listing. Size reflects PageRank, a measure of how central a node is.

The page opens on the Diocese of Baltimore, the oldest American see, because the whole church (about 10,700 people and institutions) is far too large to draw: cut down to its 500 most central nodes it is mostly disconnected fragments. Choose another diocese, or “All dioceses” to see the most central 1,000 nodes nationally (those left without a link are dropped). The panel note says how many are shown.
