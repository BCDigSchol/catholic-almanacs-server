Each point is one institution as the almanac recorded it in the selected year, placed at its geocoded town. Institutions whose place could not be located are counted in the summary but not drawn.

In the Points and Clusters displays, click a point to open the institution's record page. Name, type and diocese filters match any part of the recorded value, so “Patrick” finds every St. Patrick's.

Years with no almanac data (the 1862–63 Civil War gap, and years not yet transcribed) are skipped by the player.
