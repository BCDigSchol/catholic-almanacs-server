How this works

Write on the left; the right shows the page as readers will see it. Page details holds the title, authors, date and abstract, which the site sets above the text. When you are done, Export for the team downloads a zip of the page with its pictures; send it to the team, who drop it into the site.

Things you can put in a page

  • Picture adds a figure from your computer; select it to set its caption, alt text and width. Pictures travel with the page.
  • Record finds an institution, person or county and inserts a live card (as of a year) or a link.
  • Visualization opens a dashboard here; set it up, then Use this view to embed it exactly as it stands.
  • Footnote adds a numbered note at the cursor; notes are gathered at the end. Write the bibliography yourself under a heading of its own.
  • Layout wraps the paragraph the cursor is in: a pull quote, a note, two columns, or a picture beside its text (put the picture first).

Revising a page

Open… lists the site's pages; opening one loads its text and pictures. Export the revision and send it as you would a new page.

Your work is kept in this browser as a draft as you type (pictures excepted). See the worked example at Writing a page for this site.
