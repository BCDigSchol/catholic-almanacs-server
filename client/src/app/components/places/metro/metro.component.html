About this boundary

This area is drawn for this site; it is not one the people of the time would have recognised. The census defined no metropolitan areas before 1910, and this boundary looks back from today's idea of a metro area. It takes {{ data.metro.name }}{{ data.metro.also.length ? ' (with ' + data.metro.also.join(' and ') + ')' : '' }}, a city of {{ data.metro.populations['1870'] | number }} in 1870, and the counties around it as their lines stood in 1870: the county holding the city and each county with a quarter or more of its area within 20 miles. Every county is counted in every year, even before its city grew, and a county near two cities goes to the nearer. Use it to gather records, not as a claim about how anyone then lived or saw the place.
