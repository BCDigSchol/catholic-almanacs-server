Each point is an institution where the almanac lists at least one person in the selected year, sized by how many; colours follow the institution's function or diocese. People at places that could not be located are not drawn.

In the Points and Clusters displays, click a point to open its record: the person's page when one person is listed there, otherwise the institution's page, which lists everyone who served there that year.

Years with no almanac data (the 1862–63 Civil War gap, and years not yet transcribed) are skipped by the player.
