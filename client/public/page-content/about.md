# About the database

The Catholic Almanacs Database reconstructs the people, institutions and
dioceses of the nineteenth-century American Catholic Church from the annual
*Catholic Almanac* directories. Each year's almanac listed the Church's
dioceses, the churches, schools, convents, hospitals and charities within them,
and the clergy and religious who served there. Read across many years, those
lists show how the Church grew, moved west and changed shape.

## How the records work

- **Institutions and people are stable identities.** An institution such as a
  parish or a college has one record, with a permanent ID (for example
  `stl.mo.0148`), however its name or details changed over time.
- **Each almanac year is recorded separately.** What an almanac printed about an
  institution in a given year (its name, type, location, diocese, the clergy
  listed there) is kept as that year's entry. On an institution or person page,
  pick a year to see that year's entry, or **All years** to see everything
  recorded, merged.
- **People are linked to the institutions they served**, year by year, as
  residents or as visiting clergy who attended a church from elsewhere.

## Reading the data

- **Names are given as the almanacs printed them.** Spellings, abbreviations and
  titles vary from year to year and are kept as recorded.
- **"Not recorded"** means the almanac listed an institution without naming it,
  often a small church or mission station.
- **Coverage varies by year.** The almanac was not published in 1862 and 1863,
  and some years are still being transcribed. The
  [Coverage dashboard](/dashboards/coverage) shows which dioceses and years are
  in the database.
- **Locations are approximate.** Places are mapped from the town, county and
  state the almanac gave, so a point marks the place, not a building.

## Exploring

- [Browse institutions](/institutions) and [browse people](/people), with
  filters for place, type, diocese, religious order and years.
- The [dashboards](/dashboards) show the collection as maps, networks and
  charts, starting with [The Almanacs at a Glance](/dashboards/overview).
