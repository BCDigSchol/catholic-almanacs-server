# How to search

## The search box

The search box in the header looks up **institutions**, **people** and
**places** by name as you type. Pick a scope beside it to limit the results
to one kind, press Enter or choose *See all* to open the full list, or pick a
result to go straight to its record. Places are the counties as they were
drawn in the almanac years: searching "Kane County" or "Kane, Illinois" opens
a page gathering everything that lay within it.

## Browsing

[Institutions](/institutions) and [People](/people) list every record and
can be narrowed by name, type, diocese, state, the county *as it was* that
year, religious order and years. The institution list can also search the
almanacs' **notes** — the loose remarks printed after an entry — for any
word or "phrase". Every list can be sorted by name, year or diocese, and the
address bar carries the whole search, so a filtered list can be bookmarked
or shared.

## Reading a record

An institution or person page shows the record **as of one almanac year**:
pick a year along the top, or *All years* to see everything recorded about
it, merged. Each year keeps the name, type, place and diocese as the almanac
printed them, with the people listed there. The map band shows the record's
place and the stations and visiting clergy tied to it; *Clergy over time*
(for institutions) and *Career* (for people) follow the record across the
years. Every page has a *Cite* button with a permanent link.

## Dashboards

The [dashboards](/dashboards) show the collection as maps, networks and
charts. Start with [The Almanacs at a Glance](/dashboards/overview), where
clicking any function, type, diocese, year or map point filters every other
panel, and [Coverage](/dashboards/coverage), which shows which dioceses and
years are in the database so far. Every dashboard keeps its state in the
address bar, so a view can be bookmarked, shared or embedded in a page.

## When something looks wrong

Names, spellings and places are given **as the almanacs printed them**; see
[What the data holds](/pages/almanacs/the-data) for how they were encoded. If
you spot an error, [let us know](/pages/almanacs/contact).
