# Contact

## Getting in touch

For questions about the project's creation, maintenance or data, contact
**Ashlyn Stewart**, [stewarfu@bc.edu](mailto:stewarfu@bc.edu).

For questions about using the site or its data, particularly for research or
teaching, contact **Seth Meehan**, [meehanse@bc.edu](mailto:meehanse@bc.edu).

You can also write to the Digital Scholarship Group at
[digitalscholarship@bc.edu](mailto:digitalscholarship@bc.edu), or submit to
our [Google Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeS-_LDe8PAZRRHNGp4RhAK-QYkKcTzGGjbt37kkK6rNIKmXg/viewform?usp=header).

## Letting us know about problems

If you've noticed something incorrect in our dataset, please fill out our
[Google Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeS-_LDe8PAZRRHNGp4RhAK-QYkKcTzGGjbt37kkK6rNIKmXg/viewform?usp=header)
or send us a message so we can correct it as soon as possible.
