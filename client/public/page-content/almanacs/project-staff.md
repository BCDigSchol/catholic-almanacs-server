# Project staff

## Project leadership

**Ashlyn Stewart, PhD**, founding co-director and project editor, is a
digital scholarship specialist for the Boston College Libraries' Digital
Scholarship Group. Ashlyn serves as project manager, trains and oversees a
team of research assistants to extract and structure the almanacs data, and
creates visualizations with the data.

**Seth Meehan, PhD**, founding co-director, is the associate director of
academic and programming and special projects for the Boston College
Libraries. Seth works with stakeholders across and beyond the university to
identify opportunities for data from the almanacs to advance research.

## Web and data development team

**Joanna Schroeder** serves as the lead on project data standardization,
organization and management. She also developed the essential scripting
pipelines to 1) create IDs for individuals named in the almanac and 2) copy
coordinates for locations across multiple years.

**Dave Thomas** consults on web development and infrastructure.

Additional Digital Scholarship Group staff members **Melanie Hubbard** and
**Antonio LoPiano** contribute to project design, web design and
development, and GIS consulting.
